Loadsheet Pro – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Loadsheet Pro is a weight & balance calculator for A320 and A330 family aircraft, provided to authorised company users for iPad and Mac.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. There is no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Access code

The first time you open the app you enter your work email and a personal access code. Both are checked on your device only and are never sent anywhere. Your email is kept on the device so the app stays unlocked.

Data that stays on your device

Values you enter (weights, fuel, registration, flight details) and your settings (such as kg/lb and default taxi fuel) are kept only on your own device so the app can work. Deleting the app removes them.

Network use

When online, the app downloads the latest version of the calculator and a list of access codes that have been withdrawn. These requests send no personal information; the check against the list happens on your device. PDFs you create are saved or shared only where you choose.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated before that version is released.
